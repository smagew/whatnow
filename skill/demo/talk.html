What to try

1. The window starts folded into one line with the last message. Click the line: it opens.

2. Click the switch (the mark on the left of the line or of the title): Messages becomes Terminal in the same place and of the same size. Click it again: Messages are back. It works on the folded line too.

3. “Full width” puts the window along the bottom of the page; “Fold” makes it one line again. Both views share the size and the place, so the terminal folds into a line just like the chat.

4. In the dark bar on top choose “is open in iTerm” and “no terminal (browser)” to see what the Terminal view says when the session cannot be attached, and how the window looks for somebody who never needs a terminal.
