The bar on the left, easier to read

What changes in every variant

  • Sharp text. Light text on a dark background is drawn heavier by macOS; the new bar turns that off (grayscale smoothing), uses the regular weight, and drops the stroke that made the open row look bold.
  • One accent, not three. The open work is a quiet lighter row instead of a white block; a new reply is an amber dot and an amber word instead of a filled amber row.
  • Line icons instead of emoji, in the menu and for the state.
  • Less text. The long status sentence under every work becomes a dot and one or two words on the right.
  • Alerts and Motion move to the bottom as two small buttons.

The names of the works here are made up. Pick a variant at the top and compare with "Now".
